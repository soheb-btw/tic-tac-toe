extension How To Play Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

01

The Board Structure

The game features a 3x3 main board, where each cell contains another 3x3 sub-grid.

02

Targeted Movement

Your move in a sub-grid forces your opponent to make their next move in the matching sub-grid on the main board.

03

Winning Sub-Grids

Get 3-in-a-row inside a sub-grid to claim that entire cell on the main board for Player 1 (⭕) or Player 2 (❌).

04

Free Moves

If you are sent to a sub-grid that is already won or full, you gain a Free Move to play anywhere open!

💡 Pro Tip: Hover over any box to preview which sub-grid your move will send your opponent to!
